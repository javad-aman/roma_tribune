# Roma Tribune — Project Overview

## Mission

Roma Tribune is an independent, English-language newsletter about the Italian football club AS Roma. It is published on Substack and written for the English-speaking Roma supporter — the diaspora fan, the ex-pat, the American or British supporter who bleeds giallorosso but is underserved by coverage that is either behind an Italian paywall or reduced to wire-copy aggregation.

The goal is not to break news. The goal is to curate the best Italian-language reporting, translate it into clear English, and wrap it in a personal, opinionated voice that treats the reader like a fellow fan rather than a passive consumer.

## Audience

- English-speaking Roma supporters outside Italy
- Fans who cannot read Italian press fluently but want the perspective it provides
- Supporters tired of generic football aggregation with no personality

## Voice

Opinionated, not neutral. The newsletter takes positions — on transfers, on tactics, on management decisions. It does not invent facts or manufacture drama, but it does not hedge every sentence into meaninglessness either. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly. Opinions are stated as opinions. The tone is that of a knowledgeable, passionate fan writing for other knowledgeable, passionate fans.

## Differentiators

1. **Italian source intelligence** — Il Romanista, Corriere dello Sport, Gazzetta, Forzaroma, and the club's own channels are the primary material; English outlets and transfer reporters on X fill in the gaps.
2. **Translation layer** — Italian reporting is translated and summarized so English-only readers get access to primary sources.
3. **Personal voice** — Not a wire aggregator. The writing has a point of view.
4. **Recency discipline** — Each issue covers only what is new. Stories already covered are not recycled unless there is a material new development.

## Goals

- Publish one issue per week on Substack
- Build a loyal readership among English-speaking Roma supporters
- Over time, automate the gather and summarize steps via the Anthropic API so the owner can focus on editing and writing rather than research
